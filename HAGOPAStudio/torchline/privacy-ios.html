Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · 한국어

Controller: BUKSAETONG
Contact: cs-hagopa@buksaetong.com
This policy covers the iOS version distributed through the App Store.

Overview

Torchline is a paid game for iPhone developed and supported by HAGOPA Studio, BUKSAETONG's game studio branch. We do not collect personal information through the app. The game shows no advertising, does not track you, uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDK, has no in-app purchases, does not require an account with us, and does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Gameplay progress and settings are stored only on your device in the game's save file. We do not receive this data.
  • The game is played offline. It does not send your gameplay data to our servers.

Purchases

Torchline is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy.

Support Requests

If you contact us, we receive the email address and message you send. We use them only to respond to your request.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not show ads or use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across apps.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Torchline is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support request, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Your Choices

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete the app or its data.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete them.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when we publish a new version.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact BUKSAETONG at cs-hagopa@buksaetong.com.
