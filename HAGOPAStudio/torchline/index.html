One Torch, Four Places in Line

Your party fights in a four-slot formation, and every skill has places it can be used from and places it can reach. Whoever carries the single torch lights up the enemy line and unlocks a torch skill, and becomes the enemy's favorite target.

A Journey Through the Long Night

Four acts of story with branching routes across ashen wheat fields, misty marshes, ruined walls and a desert of cinders. Light lamp pillars, help townsfolk, and face the guardians of the night.

Made for iPhone

Touch controls designed for the phone, auto-battle and battle speed options, and 13 languages. Play offline with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no account. About five hours for one journey.
