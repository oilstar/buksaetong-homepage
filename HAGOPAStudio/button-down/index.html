Button Down

You are a button lost in a drawer. Arm yourself with paperclips, thumbtacks, coins and button batteries, and hold out against waves of dust and crumbs. The run ends when the boss falls, not when a timer runs out. Button Down is coming to Steam for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

The Boss Ends the Run

A chapter is the run. Its length, its intensity, and the boss waiting at the end are fixed, so replaying a chapter gives you the same fight and skill is what changes the result.

Small Things, Real Weapons

Fourteen weapons and eight supplies, every weapon with an evolved form. A run holds six weapons and four supplies, so the question is always what to give up.

Everything on a Clock

Trash arrives on a schedule and elites repeat on a period, where the period is the tier. Bosses scatter, sweep, and spin the horde back at you.
