Lead a guild master and a party of heroes into tactical, turn-based card battles. Chain hits on a single target to trigger devastating combo bonuses.

Run a Guild on the Brink

Recruit heroes, build and upgrade facilities, and manage wages, injuries, and permadeath as you hold back the end of the world one expedition at a time.

Descend, Again and Again

Discover and combine a deep card pool across many heroes, face distinct bosses, collect divine relics, and push endless and daily challenge runs.
