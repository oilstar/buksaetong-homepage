Ten Companions, One Hand

Every companion draws from their own deck into one shared hand. Chain combos between allies, strike weaknesses to break the enemy's guard, and spend shared Focus on devastating finishers.

Every Name Goes in the Ledger

A story told like a novel, quest by quest, across 19 regions of a branching expedition map. Companions who travel together grow closer and, in time, fight as one. No permadeath.

Made for iPhone

Tap a card to preview it, tap again to play. Press and hold anything for details. 7 languages. Play offline with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no account.
